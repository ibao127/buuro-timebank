Frequently Asked Questions

What is Buuro? ↓

Buuro is a neighbourhood timebank where people exchange time instead of money. Every hour of help you give earns you one Uuro.

What is an Uuro? ↓

An Uuro is the time currency used in Buuro. One Uuro equals one hour of help given or received.

How do I earn Uuros? ↓

You earn Uuros by helping your neighbours. Once a task is confirmed, the Uuros are transferred to your account automatically.

Can my balance go negative? ↓

Yes, up to -120 minutes. This allows new members to request help before they've had a chance to give any.

What is time-banking? ↓

Time banking means coming together as community to mutually help each other out. We explain what time-banking is in more detail on the "about" page.

Is time-banking safe? ↓

We have a review system in place where any interactions that felt unsafe can be reported and users can be banned from the online platform. Additionally, you can start time-banking by joining our in-person events first to get to know some of the people involved and build a trusted circle of time-bankers in a safe environment.

To view how other time-bankers perceived certain users, you can quickly view the star-rating on their profile. This gives you a general idea of how this time-banker is perceived in the community.

How do I post a request? ↓

Go to the Requests page and click "Post a request". Fill in the details and set how many Uuros you're offering.
