Frequently Asked Questions

What is Buuro? ↓

Buuro is a neighbourhood timebank where people exchange time instead of money. Every hour of help you give earns you one Uuro.

What is a Uuro? ↓

A Uuro is the time currency used in Buuro. One Uuro equals one hour of help given or received.

How do I earn Uuros? ↓

You earn Uuros by helping your neighbours. Once a task is confirmed, the Uuros are transferred to your account automatically.

Can my balance go negative? ↓

Yes, up to -120 minutes. This allows new members to request help before they've had a chance to give any.

How do I post a request? ↓

Go to the Requests page and click "Post a request". Fill in the details and set how many Uuros you're offering.
