Bar La Papa - every Monday

Bar la Papa is a weekly time-banking restaurant on the wonderful Groene Veld, where surplus of food and altruism gets converted into free, warm, three-course dinners for everyone.

Het Groene Veld

Het Groene Veld is one of the last free open spaces in Amsterdam. It gives room to many artists, independent makers and community organisations. Its grounds are used to host community events such as Bar La Papa.

Buuro's Mission

Our time is maybe the most precious thing we have. What you do with your time can make a big difference for yourself and for others. At Buuro we believe that time is best used on, for, and with each other. On our website you can find neighbours that can and want to help each other.

Sometimes you need help, sometimes someone else could really use yours. That is how we create a friendly atmosphere and support within the neighbourhood. Happiness multiplies by sharing it, so go and find the neighbour that can use your help today!
